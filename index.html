Expedition log

    How to play & symbols

    Explore the 4×4 cave and bring the gold back to the bottom-left camp. A stench means the living Wumpus is in an adjacent room; a breeze means an adjacent room has a pit; glitter means gold is here. A boundary collision makes a bump. Face a direction, then shoot to send your single arrow down that corridor. Each move costs 1 point, shooting costs 10, collecting gold earns 100, returning with it earns 1000, and a fatal fall costs 1000.

    Arrow keys or WASD move and turn you. Space shoots. The AI uses only visited-room percepts and its own deductions; its risky fallback is not guaranteed to survive.
